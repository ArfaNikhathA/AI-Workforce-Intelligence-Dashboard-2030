📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes the impact of Artificial Intelligence on jobs and careers by the year 2030 using Microsoft Power BI.
The dashboard focuses on workforce trends, automation risk, AI exposure, salary analysis, education impact, and future-safe careers.

Using Power BI, I performed data cleaning, data transformation, data modeling, KPI creation, and data visualization to transform raw workforce data into actionable insights.


🎯 Business Objective
The objectives of this project are:

- Understand AI's impact on different job roles
- Analyze automation risk across professions
- Identify future-safe careers
- Explore salary trends by education level
- Support workforce planning and career decisions
- Highlight the importance of reskilling and upskilling

📈 Key Insights

✅ Medium-risk jobs represent the largest workforce segment
✅ Average salary across all jobs is approximately $89.4K
✅ AI exposure remains moderate across most job categories
✅ Higher education levels show stronger earning potential
✅ AI Engineers, Doctors, Teachers, Nurses, and Research Scientists are among the most future-resilient careers
✅ Workforce reskilling is essential to adapt to automation


💡 Business Recommendations

- Invest in workforce upskilling and reskilling initiatives
- Encourage continuous learning and digital skills development
- Focus on careers with lower automation risk
- Promote higher education and specialized expertise
- Prepare organizations for AI-driven workforce transformation
